Our Story

The journey of House Digital of Business

From Vision to Reality

Founded in 2015 in Berlin, House Digital of Business began with a clear mission: to help businesses thrive in the digital landscape through innovative, tailored solutions.

What started as a small team of passionate digital experts has grown into a full-service digital agency serving clients across Germany and beyond. Our journey has been defined by a commitment to excellence, continuous innovation, and strong client relationships.

Today, we're proud to be a trusted partner for businesses seeking to enhance their digital presence, streamline operations, and drive growth through technology.
